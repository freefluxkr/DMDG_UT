### [Scene 1] 오프닝 (지붕 위 암살자들)

Three assassins dressed in black robes and masks are running swiftly and stealthily across the tiled roof like wild animals. Their clothes flutter in the night wind. Noir thriller, sumukhwa ink style, high tension, cinematic.

> **Prompt:** A slow dolly-in shot of a young Joseon King sitting in the middle of a dark, enclosed palace room. He is fully dressed in red royal robes (Gonryongpo) and a king's hat. He is wide awake, gripping a sharp traditional sword tightly on his lap, his knuckles turning white. A single flickering candle provides extreme Rembrandt lighting, casting half his face in deep shadow. Shadows of tree branches outside move like monsters on the paper doors. Suspenseful.

Suddenly, a quick zoom-in and camera shake as a long, sharp silver sword violently pierces through the paper door and thrusts into the room. Metal glinting in the dark, highly dynamic.

Royal robes fluttering gracefully. Brief slow-motion impact (time-ramp). Action movie style.
